- Three more appearances, all AppCompat-based (pre-Material 3): **Old Android** (gingerbread green on black, sharp corners), **Holo** (holo blue on black, sharp corners) and **Compact** (a denser Material 3 with tighter corners and a smaller type scale). Pick them in Settings → Appearance; the window and system bars follow through AppCompat themes, and the content follows through the Compose theme.
